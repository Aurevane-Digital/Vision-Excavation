FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How fast can you provide a free quote?+

We typically visit your site within 24 to 48 hours of contact. After reviewing civil drawings and site conditions, we deliver a comprehensive itemized estimate within 2 business days.

Do you handle municipal excavation and utility permits?+

Yes. We coordinate Ontario One Call utility locates before any tooth touches the ground, and assist with municipal road-cut, entrance, and sewer connection permits across our service territories.

What types of retaining walls do you build?+

We specialize in natural armor stone walls, precast Redi-Rock/engineered commercial block walls, and compacted gabion systems, all installed with free-draining granular backfill and geogrid reinforcement.

Are you fully insured and WSIB compliant?+

Absolutely. Vision Excavation carries full WSIB clearance for all crew members on-site along with $5,000,000 in commercial general liability insurance. Proof of coverage is supplied with every contract.
