## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, heavy-industry aesthetic tailored for high-end residential, commercial, and civil excavation projects. The visual narrative rejects generic blue-collar construction tropes in favor of precision craftsmanship, structural integrity, and modern engineering authority. 

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Engineered Precision:** Exact, grounded, and immaculate. The interface conveys the exactness of laser grading and geotechnical discipline.
- **Architectural Rawness:** Material-driven visual weight drawing inspiration from poured concrete, quarry granite, structural steel, and bedrock.
- **Uncompromising Authority:** Confident, high-ticket positioning that instills immediate trust for estate developers, custom home builders, and municipal project managers.

### Design Movement
The interface executes an **Architectural Industrial Brutalism** refined through contemporary editorial discipline. Key characteristics include:
- Structural wireframe borders referencing CAD drawings and site blueprints.
- High-contrast tonal shifts oscillating between deep obsidian/charcoal surfaces and tactile stone-aggregate light planes.
- Monolithic typography juxtaposed against technical data markers, coordinate systems, and survey tags.
- Deliberate, generous negative space that treats site photography and technical specifications like architectural monographs.

## Colors

The color architecture is rooted in geological strata and heavy equipment mechanics. The primary surface default is dark mode (`#121416`), framing earthworks with gallery-grade gravitas while maintaining high functional clarity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#D9531E` - Hi-Vis Signal Orange):** Inspired by site survey stakes, laser levels, and heavy machinery safety markings. Used strictly for critical interaction points, active states, conversion pathways, and technical focal points. Never used for large structural backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#E2DFD8` - Limestone Aggregate):** A warm, tactile architectural light tone used for prominent headlines on dark substrates, inverse content blocks, quote cards, and editorial contrast panels.
- **Tertiary (`#282B2E` - Poured Basalt):** Mid-tone slate grey deployed for surface elevation steps, structural panel borders, table dividers, and subtle component backgrounds.
- **Neutral Base (`#121416` - Deep Bedrock):** Near-black charcoal baseline that absorbs ambient glow, eliminating harsh glare while maintaining deep contrast against technical lines.

### Functional Roles
- **Surface Level 0 (Ground Canvas):** `#121416`
- **Surface Level 1 (Trench & Cards):** `#1A1D20`
- **Surface Level 2 (Machined Surfaces):** `#22262A`
- **Border Structural Rule:** `#2D3237` (Subtle), `#D9531E` (Active/Keyed)
- **Text High-Emphasis:** `#F5F4F0`
- **Text Medium-Emphasis:** `#9EA3A8`
- **Status / Precision Validations:** Success: `#4E805B` (Sage Spruce); Warning: `#D9941E` (Amber Beacon); Error: `#C93B2B` (Critical Iron Oxide).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear tensions between architectural force, technical accuracy, and effortless legibility.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Barlow Condensed (Display & Headlines):** Set uppercase or title case for prominent titles. Condensed metrics project structural scale without consuming excessive vertical space. Use tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `0em`) to mimic stamped steel and structural steel marking stamps.
- **Inter (Body Text):** Provides neutral, utilitarian balance. Preserves absolute legibility across project descriptions, technical earthwork specifications, and client proposals. Keep line lengths constrained to 65–75 characters maximum.
- **Space Mono (Labels, Metadata, Data Points):** Strictly applied to technical badges, coordinates, site elevations, scope indices, and metric indicators. Transforms mundane metadata into technical survey telemetry. Always render in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are treated like precision engineering grids with disciplined rhythm and architectural scaffolding.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid locked to a max width of `1440px`. Column gutter is `2.5rem` (`40px`), outer screen margin is `4rem` (`64px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. Gutter is `1.5rem` (`24px`), outer margin is `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutter is `1rem` (`16px`), outer margin is `1.25rem` (`20px`).

### Layout Rhythm
- Section vertical spacing follows high-magnitude architectural pacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish authoritative breathing room between major earthwork capabilities and project case studies.
- Components leverage internal spacing scales anchored around `8px` steps. Content blocks align flush with explicit 1px technical demarcation borders rather than relying on floating islands.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects soft floating shadows and diffuse skeuomorphic blurs. Depth is achieved via **Tonal Stepping**, **Architectural Layering**, and **Low-Contrast Technical Wireframes**.

### Depth Techniques
1. **The Stratified Bedrock Model (Tonal Hierarchy):**
   - **Canvas Base:** `#121416` (Deepest depth level)
   - **Excavated/Embedded Surfaces:** `#17191C` with inset hairline stroke `#24282D`
   - **Elevated Interactive Tiles:** `#1E2125` with top hairline highlight `#32383F`
   - **Inverted Focal Blocks:** `#E2DFD8` (Solid aggregate panel) overlaid on dark foundations to pull paramount information forward instantly.

2. **Technical Borders Over Drop Shadows:**
   - Elements demarcate boundaries with razor-sharp 1px rules (`#282C31`). 
   - Hover states trigger a transition to `#D9531E` (Hi-Vis Orange) or `#E2DFD8` outlines rather than expanding shadow blurs.

3. **Subtle Tactile Shadowing (Operational Equipment Only):**
   - Used sparingly on interactive floating drawers and sticky conversion anchors: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px #282C31`. No tinted diffusion; shadows are cold, heavy, and compressed.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Absolute Sharpness (`roundedness: 0`)**. Corner radii are strictly `0px` across the entire ecosystem.

### Architectural Rationale
- **Structural Integrity:** Poured concrete foundations, trench cuts, precision-drilled blasting grids, and commercial foundations possess exact, machined right angles. Soft pill buttons and rounded corners erode the perceived industrial power and mechanical precision of heavy civil earthmoving.
- **Cadence:** Rectilinear forms enable modular, CAD-like interlocking component systems where borders cleanly share lines and panels lock together with zero dead space.
- **Angled Notches (Chamfers):** For specialized status markers or high-level equipment tags, components may incorporate a single 45-degree corner chamfer (cut corner: `4px` or `8px`) created via CSS `clip-path` to evoke military and industrial machinery plates.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Industrial CTA (High-Impact Conversion):** Solid `#D9531E` background, `#121416` bold Space Mono text, all-caps, tracking `0.08em`. Border: 1px solid `#D9531E`. Padding: `16px 32px`. Radius: `0px`. Hover: Background shifts to `#E2DFD8` with `#121416` text. Active: Scale `0.98` with sharp linear transition (`100ms`).
- **Secondary Architectural Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#3C4249`, text `#E2DFD8`. Hover: Border `#D9531E`, text `#D9531E`, background `rgba(217, 83, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Tertiary Utility Action:** Text-only with leading monospace symbol (`[+]` or `//`), uppercase, Space Mono, accompanied by a dynamic orange underline on hover.

### Badges & Technical Chips
- **Geometry:** Pure rectangular geometry (`0px` radius).
- **Styling:** Monospace label (`label-sm`), uppercase, tracking `0.12em`. Background `#1A1D20`, border: 1px solid `#282C31`, text `#9EA3A8`.
- **Active / Verified Status:** Left-anchored 6px square indicator in `#D9531E`, text `#E2DFD8`, border `#D9531E`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Structural Blueprint:** Outer container set to `#1A1D20` framed by a 1px `#282C31` border. 
- **Header Slot:** Technical meta-strip displaying project coordinates (e.g., `LAT 44.2312 // LON -76.4860 // KINGSTON ON`), category badge, and equipment tier separated by structural hairlines.
- **Imagery:** High-contrast, desaturated or deep-graded photography. On hover, imagery gains slight scale (`1.02`) constrained inside a `calc(100% - 2px)` overflow container.
- **Footer Slot:** Specs displayed in two-column key-value metrics using `Space Mono`.

### Form Input Fields & Selects
- **Base Style:** Sharp rectangular inputs with `#14171A` background and `#282C31` borders.
- **Labeling:** Floating or top-stacked `Space Mono` micro-labels (`label-md`) in `#9EA3A8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px crisp border in `#D9531E`. Zero outer glow or fuzzy drop-shadow rings. An internal top-right coordinate mark or corner tick denotes active cursor capture.
- **Placeholder:** `#4E545C`, sentence case.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp `16px x 16px` squares. Border: 1px solid `#3C4249`. Unchecked: transparent fill. Checked: filled solid `#D9531E` with an inset black checkmark glyph or center square.
- **Radios:** Maintained as sharp diamonds (`transform: rotate(45deg)`) or nested squares, completely avoiding circular primitives to preserve industrial geometry.

### Machine Spec Sheets & Data Lists
- **Structure:** Monospaced tabular alignment. Alternating rows with extremely subtle surface zebra striping (`#121416` / `#16191C`).
- **Dividers:** 1px dashed or solid `#24282D` line rules.
- **Data Points:** Keys rendered in `#9EA3A8` (`body-sm`), numerical values rendered in `#F5F4F0` (`Space Mono`).

### Custom Specialized Components
- **Site Grade / Elevation Indicator:** A horizontal technical measurement bar with millimetric tick marks visualizing project scale, soil depth, or rock excavation metrics.
- **Project Dispatch Status Banner:** A heavy industrial alert bar featuring a warning chevron pattern (`striped dark slate and orange`) signaling current equipment availability and seasonal project booking windows.
